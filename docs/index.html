About This Course

This course provides a systematic treatment of international trade theory and policy, with particular emphasis on India’s agricultural trade. We begin with the classical foundations of Mercantilism, Absolute Advantage, and Comparative Advantage, and progress through modern trade theory (Heckscher–Ohlin, Stolper–Samuelson, Rybczynski), trade policy instruments (tariffs, quotas, subsidies, dumping), and open-economy macroeconomics (Balance of Payments, Foreign Exchange, Mundell–Fleming).

The second half of the course focuses on international institutions: the WTO, the Agreement on Agriculture, SPS/TBT measures, and India’s own export infrastructure (APEDA, MPEDA, DGFT). All lectures use recent Indian trade data and are illustrated with R/ggplot2 figures.

Classic Papers

A curated list of seminal works in international trade theory and policy.

Trade Theory Foundations
Ricardo, D. (1817) On the Principles of Political Economy and Taxation, Ch. 7 Origin of comparative advantage
Heckscher, E. F. (1919) "The Effect of Foreign Trade on the Distribution of Income" Ekonomisk Tidskrift — foundational factor-proportions paper
Stolper, W. F. & Samuelson, P. A. (1941) "Protection and Real Wages" Review of Economic Studies 9(1)
Samuelson, P. A. (1948) "International Trade and the Equalisation of Factor Prices" Economic Journal 58(230) — factor price equalization
Leontief, W. (1953) "Domestic Production and Foreign Trade: The American Capital Position Re-Examined" Proc. American Philosophical Society 97(4) — the Leontief Paradox
Rybczynski, T. M. (1955) "Factor Endowment and Relative Commodity Prices" Economica 22(88)
Dornbusch, Fischer & Samuelson (1977) "Comparative Advantage, Trade, and Payments in a Ricardian Model with a Continuum of Goods" AER 67(5)
Krugman, P. R. (1980) "Scale Economies, Product Differentiation, and the Pattern of Trade" AER 70(5) — New Trade Theory
Melitz, M. J. (2003) "The Impact of Trade on Intra-Industry Reallocations and Aggregate Industry Productivity" Econometrica 71(6) — firm heterogeneity
Empirical Tests
MacDougall, G. D. A. (1951) "British and American Exports: A Study Suggested by the Theory of Comparative Costs" Economic Journal 61(244) — first Ricardian test
Balassa, B. (1963) "An Empirical Demonstration of Classical Comparative Cost Theory" Review of Economics and Statistics 45(3)
Frankel, J. A. & Romer, D. H. (1999) "Does Trade Cause Growth?" AER 89(3) — IV strategy using geography
Rose, A. K. (2004) "Do We Really Know That the WTO Increases Trade?" AER 94(1) — gravity model and WTO
Trade Policy
Bhagwati, J. N. (1958) "Immiserizing Growth: A Geometrical Note" Review of Economic Studies 25(3)
Corden, W. M. (1966) "The Structure of a Tariff System and the Effective Protective Rate" Journal of Political Economy 74(3)
Brander, J. A. & Spencer, B. J. (1985) "Export Subsidies and International Market Share Rivalry" Journal of International Economics 18(1) — strategic trade policy
Krugman, P. R. (1987) "Is Free Trade Passe?" Journal of Economic Perspectives 1(2)
Bagwell, K. & Staiger, R. W. (1999) "An Economic Theory of GATT" AER 89(1) — why countries negotiate trade deals
Singer, H. W. (1950) "The Distribution of Gains between Investing and Borrowing Countries" AER 40(2) — Prebisch-Singer hypothesis
Open-Economy Macroeconomics
Mundell, R. A. (1963) "Capital Mobility and Stabilization Policy Under Fixed and Flexible Exchange Rates" Canadian Journal of Economics 29(4)
Fleming, J. M. (1962) "Domestic Financial Policies Under Fixed and Under Floating Exchange Rates" IMF Staff Papers 9(3)
Dornbusch, R. (1976) "Expectations and Exchange Rate Dynamics" Journal of Political Economy 84(6) — overshooting model
Meese, R. A. & Rogoff, K. (1983) "Empirical Exchange Rate Models of the Seventies" Journal of International Economics 14(1) — random walk benchmark
Indian Trade & Agricultural Policy
Panagariya, A. (2004) "India's Trade Reform" India Policy Forum 1(1) — comprehensive overview of liberalisation
Gulati, A. & Narayanan, S. (2003) "Subsidy Syndrome in Indian Agriculture" Economic and Political Weekly 38(27)
Henson, S. & Caswell, J. (1999) "Food Safety Regulation: An Overview of Contemporary Issues" Food Policy 24(6) — SPS and trade
Otsuki, Wilson & Sewadeh (2001) "Saving Two in a Billion: Quantifying the Trade Effect of European Food Safety Standards" Food Policy 26(5)
