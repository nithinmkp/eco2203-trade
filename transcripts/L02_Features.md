## Key Concepts

- **Intra-industry trade (IIT):** Two-way trade in similar products (e.g., cars for cars)
- **Grubel-Lloyd index:** GL = 1 - |X - M| / (X + M); ranges 0 (pure inter-industry) to 1 (pure IIT)
- **Trade openness ratio:** (X + M) / GDP — India rose from ~14% (1990) to ~44% (2012), settled ~40% (2024)
- **Lall (2000) classification:** Primary, resource-based, low/medium/high technology manufactures
