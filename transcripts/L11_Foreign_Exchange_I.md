## Key Concepts

- **Nominal exchange rate (E):** Units of domestic currency per unit of foreign currency
- **Real exchange rate (q):** q = E × P*/P — competitiveness measure
- **PPP (absolute):** E = P/P* (law of one price applied to price levels)
- **PPP (relative):** % change in E = inflation differential (π - π*)
- **UIP:** i = i* + E[ΔE]/E (expected exchange rate change compensates interest differential)
- **Overshooting:** Exchange rate overshoots long-run value on impact of a monetary shock, due to sluggish prices
