## Key Concepts

- **Comparative Advantage:** Country A has CA in good X if its opportunity cost of X is lower than country B's
- **Labour productivity (a_LC):** Units of labour per unit of commodity C in country i
- **Autarky price ratio:** p_X/p_Y = a_LX/a_LY (relative labour requirements)
- **Gains from trade:** Both countries can consume beyond their PPF
- **MacDougall Test:** If US/UK wage ratio = w, industries where US labour productivity ratio > w should be US net exporters

---

## MacDougall (1951) Key Finding

Using 1937 data for 25 industries:
- US wages were roughly **twice** UK wages
- Industries where US output/worker was > 2× UK → US had higher export share
- Result strongly supported comparative advantage predictions
