## Key Concepts

- **Market access pillar:** Tariffication of non-tariff barriers; bound tariff reductions; TRQs
- **Domestic support pillar:** AMS = (administered price - reference price) × quantity; reduce trade-distorting support
- **Export competition pillar:** Eliminate export subsidies (achieved 2015 Nairobi)
- **Green Box:** Non-trade-distorting support (decoupled payments, research, extension) — no limit
- **Blue Box:** Production-limiting program payments — exempt with conditions
- **Amber Box:** Trade-distorting (price support, input subsidies) — subject to AMS disciplines
- **De minimis:** AMS < 5% of production value (developed), < 10% (developing) — exempt
- **Peace Clause (Bali 2013):** India's MSP food procurement programmes protected from AMS challenge until permanent solution found
