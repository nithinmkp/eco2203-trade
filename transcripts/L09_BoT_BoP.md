## Key Concepts

- **Current Account (CA):** Exports - Imports of goods and services + primary income + secondary income (remittances)
- **Financial Account (FA):** Net acquisition of foreign assets (FDI, FPI, loans)
- **CA + FA = 0** (with official reserves changes)
- **Marshall–Lerner (ML) condition:** |ε_X| + |ε_M| > 1 → devaluation improves trade balance
- **J-curve:** Trade balance worsens initially after devaluation (import prices rise immediately; export volumes adjust slowly)
