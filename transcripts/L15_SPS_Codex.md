## Key Concepts

- **SPS measure:** Any measure applied to protect human, animal, or plant life/health from food-borne risks, pests, or diseases
- **TBT measure:** Technical standards and regulations not covered by SPS
- **Codex Alimentarius Commission (CAC):** Joint FAO/WHO body; sets MRLs, food safety standards
- **OIE:** World Organisation for Animal Health — animal disease standards
- **IPPC:** International Plant Protection Convention — plant pest standards
- **Equivalence:** SPS Agreement Art. 4 — exporter can demonstrate its measures achieve the same level of protection
- **Otsuki et al. (2001) finding:** EU raising aflatoxin standard from 9 ppb (Codex) to 4 ppb cut African groundnut exports by 64%
