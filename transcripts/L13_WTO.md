## Key Concepts

- **MFN (Most Favoured Nation):** Tariff concession to one member must be extended to all members
- **National Treatment:** Imported goods must be treated no less favourably than domestic goods once imported
- **Bound tariff:** Maximum committed tariff rate (can be lower in practice = "applied tariff")
- **Bagwell & Staiger (1999):** Countries join GATT/WTO to escape a "terms-of-trade prisoner's dilemma" — unilateral optimal tariffs result in mutually harmful tariff wars
- **DSU:** Formal process — consultations → panel → Appellate Body → authorised retaliation
