## Key Concepts

- **Gains from trade:** Output or welfare improvement from specialisation and exchange
- **Merchandise trade:** Physical goods crossing borders (DGCI&S classification)
- **Services trade:** Invisibles — software, travel, remittances (RBI BoP)
- **Trade openness:** (Exports + Imports) / GDP × 100
