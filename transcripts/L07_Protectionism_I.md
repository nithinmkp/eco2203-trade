## Key Concepts

- **Tariff:** Tax on imports; specific ($/unit) or ad valorem (% of price)
- **Consumer surplus loss:** Areas a + b + c + d (under tariff)
- **Producer surplus gain:** Area a
- **Government revenue:** Area c
- **Net welfare loss (deadweight):** Areas b + d
- **ERP:** (VA_d - VA_w) / VA_w × 100 — measures protection of value added, not just output price
- **Quota:** Quantitative limit on imports; equivalent to tariff under competition, non-equivalent under monopoly

## Welfare Areas (Salvatore notation)

```
Price
  |      S_domestic
  |         /
  |  a  b  / c  d
P_T|-----/----------  P_tariff
P_W|----/------------  P_world
  |   /
  |  D
  |__________________ Quantity
```

- **a:** redistribution from consumers to producers
- **b + d:** deadweight loss (production and consumption distortion)
- **c:** government tariff revenue
