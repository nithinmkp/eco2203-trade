## Key Concepts

- **Sunk costs of exporting:** Market research, buyer relationships, compliance certification — explains why only high-productivity firms export (Roberts & Tybout 1997)
- **Learning-by-exporting:** Exposure to foreign competition improves firm productivity
- **Melitz (2003):** Only firms with productivity above a threshold find it profitable to export after paying sunk costs; trade liberalisation reallocates market share to high-productivity exporters → aggregate productivity rises
