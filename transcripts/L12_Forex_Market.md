## Key Concepts

- **Spot rate:** Immediate delivery (T+2 settlement)
- **Forward rate:** Agreed-upon rate for future delivery (hedging instrument)
- **Covered Interest Parity:** F/S = (1+i)/(1+i*) — no arbitrage with forward cover
- **Impossible Trinity:** Cannot simultaneously have: (1) fixed exchange rate, (2) free capital mobility, (3) independent monetary policy
- **Managed float:** RBI buys/sells USD to smooth INR volatility without targeting a fixed level
- **FEMA (1999):** Replaced FERA; liberalised current account; capital account still managed
