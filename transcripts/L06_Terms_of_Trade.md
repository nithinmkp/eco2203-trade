## Key Concepts

- **Terms of Trade (ToT):** P_X / P_M × 100 — export price index over import price index
- **Improvement in ToT:** Country gets more imports per unit of exports exported
- **Prebisch–Singer:** Long-run secular decline in primary commodity prices relative to manufactures
- **Immiserizing Growth:** Growth that expands export supply so much it worsens ToT, leaving country worse off
- **Trade Creation:** FTA replaces domestic high-cost production with cheaper imports from partner
- **Trade Diversion:** FTA diverts imports from efficient non-member to less efficient member
