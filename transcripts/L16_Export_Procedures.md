## Key Documents

| Document | Issuing Authority | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| IEC (Importer Exporter Code) | DGFT | Mandatory for all exports/imports |
| Shipping Bill | Customs (ICEGATE) | Customs clearance for export |
| Bill of Lading / Airway Bill | Shipping line / airline | Proof of shipment |
| Phytosanitary Certificate | State Agriculture Dept / NPPO | Plant health certification |
| Certificate of Origin | EPC / Chamber of Commerce | Preferential tariff claims |
| GR/SDF Form | RBI (via AD bank) | Foreign exchange declaration |

---

## Djankov et al. (2010) Key Finding

- Each additional day in export transit reduces trade by ~1%
- Developing countries lose significant export revenue due to poor logistics
- India's "time to export" fell from ~16 days (2010) to ~9 days (2023) — room for improvement
