## Key Concepts

- **Export subsidy:** Government payment to exporters; lowers world price → terms of trade worsen for large country
- **Dumping:** Selling in foreign market below domestic price or below cost of production
- **Predatory dumping:** Temporary low pricing to eliminate competition, then raise prices
- **Strategic trade policy:** Government subsidy shifts oligopoly rents from foreign to domestic firms
- **Brander–Spencer result:** In duopoly (Cournot), export subsidy increases domestic firm's equilibrium output and profits at foreign firm's expense
