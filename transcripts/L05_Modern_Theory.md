## Key Concepts

- **HO Theorem:** A country exports the good that uses its abundant factor intensively
- **Stolper–Samuelson:** Trade raises the real return of the abundant factor, lowers the scarce factor
- **Rybczynski:** At constant prices, an increase in one factor increases output of the factor-intensive good and *decreases* output of the other
- **FPE Theorem:** Free trade in goods equalises factor prices across countries under HO assumptions
- **Leontief Paradox:** US (capital-abundant) exported labour-intensive goods in 1947 — violated HO

---

## Resolving the Leontief Paradox

1. Human capital (skills) embedded in US labour
2. Natural resource intensity of US imports
3. Factor intensity reversal
4. Demand differences (home bias)
