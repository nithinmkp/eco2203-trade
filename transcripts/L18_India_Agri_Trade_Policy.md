## FTP 2023–28 Key Targets

- Merchandise + services exports target: **$2 trillion by 2030**
- 100 new Districts as Export Hubs
- E-commerce exports promotion
- Rupee trade settlement mechanism

---

## Export Restrictions — Economic Analysis

| Policy | Effect |
|---|---|
| Minimum Export Price (MEP) | Reduces export competitiveness; protects domestic consumers |
| Export ban (e.g., non-basmati rice Aug 2023) | Lowers domestic prices; may harm farmers |
| Quantitative export limits | Creates uncertainty; erodes India's reliability as supplier |
